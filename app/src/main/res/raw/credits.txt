VOICEFIXER (MODEL OF STUDIO RESTORATION)

Studio voice restoration by Haohe Liu and contributors (https://github.com/haoheliu/voicefixer). The model is not part of the app. It is downloaded when you add the stage, as converted to ONNX by Tigre Gótico (audiosronnx-voicefixer, revision aa259fa5), and can be removed again.

The model weights are Haohe Liu's "VoiceFixer Model CheckPoint", version 3 (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5600188), licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: converted to ONNX by Tigre Gótico; read by Takes with a fixed context of 256 mel frames instead of the conversion's 501, in overlapping windows mixed with the recording. No retraining.
