# Takes — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

Takes is a camera and video editor for Android. This policy explains what the app does
with your data. The short version: your videos never leave your device unless you send them.

## Data the app collects

None. Takes has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. Your
recordings, your projects and their sound are never uploaded anywhere.

## When the app uses the internet

Versions up to 0.4 do not request the Android internet permission at all.

From version 0.5 the app connects to the internet for one thing only: downloading the
speech model used for subtitles (about 161 MB). This happens only when you tap the
download button in the Subtitles tool, which shows the size first. Nothing is downloaded
when the app starts, there are no update checks and no telemetry.

The model files come from Hugging Face (`huggingface.co` and its content delivery
network). The request carries no account, cookie or identifier. As with any download, the
server sees your IP address, and that request is covered by
[Hugging Face's privacy policy](https://huggingface.co/privacy).

Subtitles are transcribed on your device. The sound of your videos is not sent to anyone.
You can remove the downloaded model from the same tool.

## Permissions

- **Camera** — to record video.
- **Microphone** — to record the audio of your video.
- **Internet** (from version 0.5) — only to download the speech model for subtitles, when
  you ask for it.

Camera and microphone are used only while you are using the recorder.

## Where your videos are stored

Recordings, projects and exported movies are saved in the app's private storage on your
device. They leave it only when you choose to: by sharing an export with another app, or
by saving it to your gallery (in `Movies/Takes`). What happens after that is governed by
the app or service you shared it with.

Uninstalling Takes deletes its private storage, including all projects. Movies you saved
to the gallery are kept.

## Children

Takes does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.
