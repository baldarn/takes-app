Takes is a camera and a video editor in one app, built around the way videos are actually made: one take at a time.

RECORD IN TAKES
• Record, pause, resume and stop: the large button stops, the round one beside it pauses. Every pause closes a take, and you can come back to the same project later, even after closing the app.
• Switch camera while recording, choose the main, ultrawide, tele or front lens, zoom presets, pinch to zoom and timed zoom transitions.
• Use two cameras: the rear and the front camera in one picture, in four layouts, on phones that run both at once.
• 1080p or 4K, HDR (HLG, ten bits), stabilisation, Level horizon, Follow face, focus lock, manual focus and focus peaking, each where your phone's camera offers it.
• Live video effects while you record, with Crash Zoom and Dolly Zoom buttons.
• Choose the microphone, watch its live level, and set a recording gain.
• Bookmarks: mark a moment while recording and jump back to it in the editor.
• Record here: new takes go into the edit at the playhead.

EDIT ON A TIMELINE
• Thumbnail timeline with waveforms, timeline zoom and full-screen preview.
• Split, trim, delete and reorder clips; mark a range to delete, copy, cut, paste or apply a tool to just that part; undo and redo that survive closing the app.
• Layers: overlapping videos as cover, side by side, top and bottom or picture in picture, and image overlays.
• Import videos and photos at the playhead, or takes you have not used yet.
• Nine transitions: dissolve, fade through a color, wipe, split screen, Hitchcock, whip pan, flash cut, still and defocus, each with its own sound timing.
• Titles that follow the footage, with looks, fonts, gradients, outline, shadow, entrance and exit motion, and saved styles.
• Picture: speed from 0.25× to 4× with the pitch kept, crop, rotation and animated reframing, 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 or 4:5, mirror and stabilisation.
• Color: fourteen looks, your own .cube LUTs, exposure, warmth, tint, highlights, color correction with keyframes, saved and animated grades, and Log footage.
• Video effects as in YouRec: Noir, Bleach Bypass, Kaleidoscope, Quantization, film textures, Chroma Key, Auto Background, Color Isolation, Crash Zoom, Dolly Zoom, Night fix and many more, with keyframes and each effect's film history.
• Sound: levels in half decibels, fades, two audio tracks for music or voice, voice recording, noise reduction with RNNoise or DeepFilterNet3, voice EQ, censor beep, and Remove pauses.
• Subtitles: speech is transcribed on your phone, with the model YouRec uses or a lighter one; correct, regroup and style the captions.
• Your original recordings are never modified.

EXPORT AND SHARE
• Export at original size, 720p, 1080p or 4K, H.264 or H.265, 24 to 60 fps, with HDR kept.
• An export pauses when you leave the app and goes on from where it stopped. The finished movie is checked before it is offered to you.
• Play it, share it, save it to your gallery, or share a frame as the cover.

YOUR VIDEOS STAY YOURS
• No account, no ads, no tracking.
• Your recordings and projects never leave your device unless you share them.
• The app goes online for one thing only: downloading the speech model for subtitles (about 987 MB or about 375 MB, depending on the model), and only when you ask.
• Deleted projects go to Recently Deleted, where you can restore them.
• Duplicate a project, or save it as a ZIP and open it again later or on another phone.
• Projects travel to and from YouRec on iPhone: Takes opens YouRec's project ZIPs, and Zip for iPhone saves one YouRec opens.
• In English and Italian.

Takes is an unofficial, open source Android port of YouRec by Salvatore Sanfilippo (antirez).
