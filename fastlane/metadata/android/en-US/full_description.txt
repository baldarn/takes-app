Takes is a camera and a video editor in one app, built around the way videos are actually made: one take at a time.

RECORD IN TAKES
• Record, pause, resume and stop: the large button stops, the round one beside it pauses. Every pause closes a take, and you can come back to the same project later, even after closing the app.
• Switch between front and rear camera while recording.
• Zoom presets, pinch to zoom, and smooth timed zoom transitions. Each camera remembers its own zoom.
• Tap to focus, focus lock and manual focus, 4K, mirrored selfies and a front light, each where your phone's camera offers it.
• Recorder settings and a quick panel.
• Choose the microphone and an order of preference among microphones, and watch the one in use and its live level at the top of the screen.
• Controls that turn with the phone.
• Bookmarks: mark a moment while recording and jump back to it in the editor.

EDIT ON A TIMELINE
• Scrolling thumbnail timeline with audio waveforms.
• Split at the playhead, trim with handles, delete and reorder clips.
• Ranges and selection: mark a start and an end, preview the cut, then delete, copy, cut and paste, or apply a tool to just that part.
• Jump to the previous or next cut, with a magnet that snaps to cuts.
• Titles that follow the footage, with looks, fonts, gradients, outline, shadow, background, entrance and exit motion, and styles you can save.
• Nine transitions between clips and at the start and end: dissolve, fade through a color, wipe, split screen, Hitchcock, whip pan, flash cut, still and defocus, each with its own sound timing.
• Import videos from your device into a project.
• Sound for each clip: volume in half decibels with a warning when the mix would clip, mute, and fades of any length. Drag the waveform to set the level.
• Two separate audio tracks for music or voice, imported from your files.
• Noise reduction for recorded sound.
• Remove pauses: find the silences in a clip and cut them out.
• Color: looks, exposure, warmth and tint for each clip, or import your own .cube LUT.
• Video effects as in YouRec: Noir, Day for Night, Bleach Bypass, Kaleidoscope, Auto Background and many more, each with its film history.
• Crop and reframe the picture, rotate it, or animate between two framings.
• Add video at the playhead: record a new take there, or drop in takes you have not used yet.
• Subtitles: speech is transcribed on your phone, then you can edit the text and choose its style. Two speech models to choose from: the larger one is the model YouRec uses.
• Clip speed from 0.25× to 4×, with the pitch kept.
• Undo and redo that survive closing the app.
• Your original recordings are never modified.

EXPORT AND SHARE
• Export your edit as a movie, choosing size, H.264 or H.265 and frame rate.
• An export pauses when you leave the app or turn the screen off, and goes on from where it stopped.
• The finished movie is checked before it is offered to you.
• Play it, share it, or save it to your gallery.

YOUR VIDEOS STAY YOURS
• No account, no ads, no tracking.
• Your recordings and projects never leave your device.
• The app goes online for one thing only: downloading the speech model for subtitles (about 987 MB or about 375 MB, depending on the model), and only when you ask.
• Deleted projects go to Recently Deleted, where you can restore them.
• Duplicate a project, or export it as a ZIP and open it again later or on another phone.
• In English and Italian.

Takes is an unofficial, open source Android port of YouRec by Salvatore Sanfilippo (antirez). It is an early release: more editing tools are on the way.
